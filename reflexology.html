Reflexology Therapy

Reflexology is a complementary practice that applies pressure to selected areas, commonly on the feet or hands, with the aim of promoting relaxation. At Sri Sri Wellbeing Chennai, enquire about the session format and discuss whether it is suitable for you.

Understanding Reflexology

Reflexology sessions generally involve applying measured pressure to areas of the feet or hands. Many people choose it as a relaxation-focused complementary practice; it should not be considered a method to diagnose or cure medical conditions.

Personal Comfort and Suitability

Inform the practitioner about foot or hand injuries, skin conditions, circulation concerns, diabetes, pregnancy or other relevant health matters. Pressure and techniques should be adapted to your individual needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about Sri Sri Wellbeing Chennai, our consultations and wellness therapies.

What services are available at Sri Sri Wellbeing Chennai?

Sri Sri Wellbeing Chennai is an Ayurvedic wellness centre in Kilpauk. The centre offers Ayurveda consultations and wellness therapies, including Panchakarma, detoxification and integrated therapies. Please contact the centre to confirm current service availability.

Where is Sri Sri Wellbeing Chennai located?

The centre is located at 119/50, New Avadi Road, Alagappa Nagar, Kilpauk, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600010. Please check the Contact section or call the centre for directions.

How can I enquire about an appointment?

You can call +91 99430 13111 or use the centre’s WhatsApp link to enquire about consultations, available therapies, timings and appointments.

Do I need a consultation before starting a therapy?

A consultation with a qualified practitioner is recommended before starting a therapy. The practitioner can review your health history and discuss whether a particular therapy is appropriate.

What should I expect during a Reflexology session?

A session typically involves comfortable positioning and pressure applied to selected areas of the feet or hands. Tell the practitioner if any pressure feels uncomfortable and discuss relevant health concerns before starting.

Does the centre offer Netra Tejas or eye-related wellness services?

The centre lists Netra Tejas among its wellness services. Please contact the centre for current availability and consult a qualified practitioner to discuss suitability. Do not delay or replace eye care from an ophthalmologist for vision symptoms or concerns.

Are therapies guaranteed to cure a health condition?

No specific outcome can be guaranteed. Outcomes vary, and wellness therapies should not be presented as a guaranteed cure or a replacement for medical diagnosis or treatment. Discuss your health concerns with a qualified healthcare professional.

This information is general and does not replace an individual consultation or medical advice. Please confirm therapy suitability with a qualified practitioner.
